# Mandala

## Subject
A Buddhist mandala built from clean geometry. Concentric rings surround a square palace with four T-shaped gates; at the center sits an eight-point star around a seed-of-life rosette. Rings, from the outside in: a crown of triangles, a dotted band, a band of interlaced circles, a band of pointed lenses, then the field around the palace.

## Style
Vintage pastel print: flat color, crisp geometric edges, thin line work, a faint paper grain. Only clean geometric shapes (circles, arcs, triangles, lenses, polygons, stars). No figurative or iconographic motifs: no vajras, flames, lotus petals, flowers, or wheel. Strict fourfold and radial symmetry.

## Palette
Fills use only these six: thistle #D8BFD8, mauve #D1B1CB, taupe #CAB7A2, cream #E8D4C1, sand #E5C8A5, warm grey #C9C0BB. One deep plum #7F5B78 (a darker shade of the mauve) is used only for line work, for contrast.

## Composition
Square, on a cream ground. The mandala fills most of the canvas; quarter-circle arcs mark the corners.
