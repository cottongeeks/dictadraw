# Winter banner

## Subject
A close replica of the user's "Everything But the Book" winter header (`references/reference.png`, kept local, not committed), with the lettering removed. A medium-blue field is framed by wavy cream lines with a white six-armed star in each corner. On the left a crescent moon and scattered stars, sparkles, and snow dots sit above the empty navy title panel. A cream owl flies over the panel's top right corner, its wings dotted with small blue stars. A row of seven navy eight-point stars, each with a pale square and a ring of dots, runs along the bottom. In the middle stands a blue fir tree hung with red baubles and topped with a red-and-pink star. On the right a spotted cream deer with branching antlers looks back, between two arrow-tipped snowflakes and two big blue stars.

## Style
Nordic folk print in chalky crayon: flat shapes with soft, slightly wobbly edges and a fine grain of speckles. Nothing glossy, no outlines except the ear loops, the outline star, and the snowflakes' centers.

## Palette
- Field `#3a7cb0`, title panel navy `#134b7c`.
- Cream `#dacab4` for the moon, owl, deer, border, and most stars; white `#e9e6d8` for corner stars, the big snowflake, and some sparkles and dots.
- Motif blue `#16578e` for the tree, the big stars, the row stars, and three thin sparkles.
- Baubles red `#8c1530` with pink `#da96b5` asterisks; the tree star is red with a pink rim.

## Composition
- 1997 × 1132, the reference's pixel size; every shape keeps the reference's position and size.
- The title panel stays empty: no lettering in it.
- The bottom border is the top border moved down, and the right border is the left one moved across, as in the reference.
