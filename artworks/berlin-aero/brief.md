# Berlin Aero

## Subject
A young woman with long brown hair, wearing big glossy over-ear headphones, walking along a Berlin street. The Fernsehturm rises over a row of Altbau facades behind her. A green Ampelmännchen light, linden trees, and floating soap bubbles fill out the scene.

## Style
Frutiger Aero, the glossy optimism of early-2000s tech (Windows XP "Bliss", Vista, iPod minis, Softsoap fish bottles). Clean vector shapes with gel-like gloss: every object gets a soft white highlight band near its top and a slightly deeper tone below. Glass and chrome reflections, lens flares, translucent bubbles. Bright, airy, and clean, not gritty. A stylized fashion-illustration figure.

## Palette
Saturated deep blue to cyan sky, pale only at the horizon. Saturated colors everywhere else: avoid large white areas. Bright white clouds. Lime and grass green (`#7ed321`-ish) for trees and the Ampelmann. Aqua and teal for glass. Chrome white and silver for the headphones and the tower's sphere. From the reference outfit: pale sky-blue top and skirt with orange panels, a lime iPod-mini-style player, and clear aqua accents. Warm light skin and chestnut hair.

## Composition
Two versions of one scene. Both share the woman, the street, the trees, the Ampel, and the bubbles, and differ in framing and color:
- `panorama.ts`: a wide frieze, 1920 × 1120 (close to 16:9). She walks right, left of center, about two-thirds of the canvas tall. The Fernsehturm stands right of center to balance her, and the sun is at the top right. Candy-colored Altbau (aqua, tangerine, cobalt, lime, mint), a deep blue sky, and tinted pavement with a cobble strip.
- `square.ts`: 1:1 (1400 × 1400). She is smaller, under 60% of the height. The Fernsehturm is on the right and the sun is at the top left. Vista-style aurora ribbons sweep across the sky, the houses are aqua and green, a lime lawn strip runs along their base, and glossy aqua pavement mirrors her.

Render them with `pnpm art:render berlin-aero/panorama` and `pnpm art:render berlin-aero/square`.
