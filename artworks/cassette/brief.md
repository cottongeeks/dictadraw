# Cassette

## Subject
A compact cassette seen face on, after the user's "Nur geträumt" cinema postcard (`references/postcard.png`, kept local, not committed): black ribbed plastic body with five screws, a hot-pink label with a cream ruled writing panel, a purple band holding the dark window with both reels and the tape window, and the raised trapezoid with its four holes along the bottom.

## Style
Close replica of the postcard, flat print plus light relief: crisp vector shapes, the plastic's embossed rib grid, small bevels on the trapezoid, the holes, and the screws. No photographic glare.

## Palette
- Plastic: near-black warm grey. The photo's plastic is lit from above and reads too bright, so it is darkened to about 55% of the photo's values: slats `#3d3933`, grooves `#16130f`, rib highlights `#7d776d`.
- Label pink `#f6094f`, cream panel `#e4d5c1` with thin red rules `#c95a4f`.
- Purple band: a gradient from magenta `#c4468a` on the left to plum `#861957` on the right.
- Window `#3d3a35`, reel hubs off-white `#d8d0c0`, tape a dusty brown.
- "SEITE A" in white.

## Composition
- 2008 × 1276 (20 units per mm): the real compact-cassette ratio, 100.4 × 63.8 mm. The postcard is A6 (about 1.41:1), so its bands are shortened vertically; round parts (reels, holes, screws) keep their size.
- Texture: horizontal slats on a 20-unit pitch, crossed by 32 bright vertical ribs, everywhere on the plastic.
- Removed from the postcard: the QR code and "TRAILER ANSEHEN", the weltkino logo, the track list, "MADE IN FRANCE", "Nur geträumt", and "AB 17.12. IM KINO". Kept: "SEITE A" and the ruled lines.
