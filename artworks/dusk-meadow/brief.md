# Dusk meadow

## Subject
Rolling hills at dusk with a low sun, a lone tree on a middle hill, and tall grass and small wildflowers in the foreground.

## Style
Layered editorial landscape: flat shapes, atmospheric perspective (far hills pale and violet, near hills deep green), soft gradients only in the sky. Quiet and warm.

## Palette
Lavender to peach sky, a pale gold sun, violet-grey distant hills, sage and deep green near hills, cream and coral flowers.

## Composition
Landscape, 16:10. Horizon in the lower half. The sun sits left of center, partly set behind the far hills; the tree balances it on the right.
