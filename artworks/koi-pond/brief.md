# Koi pond

## Subject
Two koi circling each other in a dark pond, seen from directly above, with lily pads around the edges and one pink lotus in bloom.

## Style
Decorative and calm, like a Japanese textile print: clean organic silhouettes, flat color, a soft shadow under everything that floats, faint ripples.

## Palette
Deep teal water, two greens for the pads, one koi white with vermilion patches and one orange with white patches, a soft pink lotus with a gold center.

## Composition
Square. The koi form a loose circle around the center, heads chasing tails. Pads frame the corners and leave the center open.
