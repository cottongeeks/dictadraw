# Olive branch

## Subject
A single olive branch arcing from the lower left toward the upper right, with slender leaves and a small cluster of ripe olives near the middle.

## Style
A vintage botanical print: flat color, soft form shading, a faint paper grain. Calm and airy, with plenty of empty paper.

## Palette
Warm cream paper, sage leaves (some showing their silvery undersides), a brown-grey stem, olives from deep aubergine to unripe green.

## Composition
Portrait, 4:5. The branch is the only subject and sits slightly left of center; its tip leans into the upper right.
