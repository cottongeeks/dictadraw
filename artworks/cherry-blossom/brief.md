# Cherry blossom

## Subject
A cherry tree in full bloom, adapted from `references/reference.png`: a trunk at the lower left, limbs spreading up and to the right, dense clouds of pink blossom on the left thinning to scattered sprigs and drifting petals on the right.

## Style
Clean vector illustration. Blossoms are five-petal flowers with notched petal tips; branches are gnarled and taper to fine twigs.

## Palette
Depends on the variation.

## Composition
Postcard, landscape 3:2 (1500 × 1000). Weight on the left, open air on the right.

## Variations
The user picks one; it becomes `scene.ts` and the others are deleted.

- `classic`: the reference's composition and magenta-pink palette on warm white.
- `classic-hakubun`: `classic` with its seal swapped for a hakubun one after `references/seal-reference.png`: a tall vermilion block with 櫻 in seal script cut out of it.
- `moonlight`: a branch reaching in from the top left, silhouetted against a large moon in an indigo night sky; pale blossoms.
- `moonlight-seal`: `moonlight` with a small tall hakubun name seal, 芙蘭詩 in seal script, in muted vermilion in the sky at the lower right.
- `woodblock`: an ukiyo-e print: ink branch across warm paper, a red sun, a blue sky band at the top, a red seal.
- `pastel`: the reference's composition in the user's vintage pastels (thistle, mauve, taupe, cream, sand, warm grey) with plum line work; blossom clouds as soft flat shapes.
- `sumie`: sumi-e ink painting: a branch drooping in from the top right in layered ink on rice paper, sparse pink and ink-outlined blossoms, wide empty space, a red seal.
- `rinpa`: a Rinpa folding screen after Kōrin: gold-leaf squares and panel folds, a dark stylized stream with flowing gold lines, a mossy tree with flat white and pink blossoms.
- `indigo`: an aizome (indigo-dyed) textile: pale blossoms and branches on indigo, a seigaiha wave band along the bottom, a sakura kamon (crest) in the upper right.
